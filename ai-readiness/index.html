How to use this checklist
  • Complete it jointly. Designed for both IT and business leadership. Items are tagged Owner: IT, Business, or Shared so everyone knows who is responsible.
  • Start before your pilot. Use this as a pre-pilot readiness check before assigning Copilot licenses beyond a small group. That keeps risk low while you prove value.
  • Begin with where data lives. Confirm source-of-truth business data (client lists, line cards, team directory, key policies) lives in SharePoint, not individual OneDrive accounts, so it survives turnover and can be governed centrally.
  • Review permissions, then turn on Copilot. Make sure SharePoint/Teams permissions follow a simple Owners / Members / Visitors pattern mapped to real roles. Only then enable Copilot for pilot users.
  • Use this as a working document. Mark each item In Place / Needs Work / N/A, capture notes inline, and store the completed checklist in a central governance workspace as an auditable record. Revisit at least quarterly.
  • Tie it into your pilot plan. Identify a small set of pilot users and 2–3 workflows each (client follow-up emails, meeting prep, account research), and confirm the data, permissions, and policies behind them are ready before training or rollout.
